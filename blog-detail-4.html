Join Our Coffee Sharing Sessions

By Valid Team August 19, 2022 Events

Join our community sharing sessions to learn and grow with other coffee lovers. There's no better way to improve your skills than by learning from others.

Building a Community

Our workshops are designed to be interactive and accessible to everyone, from complete beginners to seasoned home baristas. Each session focuses on a specific topic, such as manual brewing methods, latte art fundamentals, or sensory training.

"Coffee brings people together. Our goal is to create a space where knowledge is shared freely and passion is ignited."

Upcoming Sessions

  • Sept 5: Manual Brewing Basics (V60 & Chemex).
  • Sept 12: Introduction to Latte Art.
  • Sept 19: The Art of Tasting (Cupping).
  • Sept 26: Home Espresso Machine Maintenance.

Spots are limited to ensuring everyone gets hands-on authentic experience. Reserve your spot early and get ready to elevate your coffee game!
