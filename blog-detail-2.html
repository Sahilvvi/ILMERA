Cold Brew Coffee: A Refreshing Guide

By Barista Jane August 19, 2022 Brewing Tips

Discover the refreshing world of cold brew and why it's becoming a favorite among coffee enthusiasts. Unlike iced coffee, cold brew is steeped for hours to extract a smoother, less acidic flavor.

The Science Behind Cold Brew

Cold brew isn't just cold coffee; it's a specific brewing method. By using cold water and a long steeping time (usually 12-24 hours), we extract different compounds from the coffee beans compared to hot brewing. The result is a concentrate that is naturally sweeter and lower in acidity.

"Patience is the secret ingredient in cold brew. The slow extraction process unlocks hidden notes in the bean that heat often destroys."

How to Make It at Home

  • Coarsely grind your favorite beans.
  • Mix with cold filtered water (1:8 ratio).
  • Steep in the fridge for 18-24 hours.
  • Strain and serve over ice.

Experiment with different beans and roast levels. Many find that medium-dark roasts work exceptionally well, providing a rich, chocolatey base that holds up well with milk or cream.
